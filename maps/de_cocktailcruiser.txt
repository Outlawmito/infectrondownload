Background: Terrorists have captured a freigher carrying an 
extremely hazardous cocktail of chemical and biological waste.
They killed the crew and navigated the cargo ship into the 
Hudson Bay, after they turned it into a "doomsday topedo".
Now they are ready to blow off the whole ship in the middle of New York City.
CT's have started the operation "cocktail-cruiser" and are raiding the
freighter.

Counter-Terrorists: Prevent Terrorists
from placing C4 to the explosive crates.
Team members must defuse any bombs 
that threaten targeted areas.

Terrorists: The Terrorist carrying the
C4 must destroy one of the two targets.
