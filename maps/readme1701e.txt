Additional info:

Based on Star Trek: First Contact by Paramount Pictures. 
The ship is not intended to be the exact replica of the one in the movie, it's more like 
an extract of some interesting parts of the ncc 1701 E
The architecture in the halls was redesigned about three times, as well as the textures.

r_speeds are very good throughout the map, never above 500 while containing a nice amount of details.

Build time:
As I stated above, this map was begun 4 months ago, but it took so long to finish because
[Kasperg] YUGO had other map projects to do. This is the second version of the ship to be released on the net.
