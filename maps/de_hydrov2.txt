Hydro Electric V2 - Bomb/Defuse
by FreakyZoid 

A terrorist faction is attempting to
disable HFN's hyrdo-electric plant by
either destroying the main generator,
or by blowing up the flow control
situated on the main bridge.

Counter-Terrorists: Prevent Terrorists
from bombing either target. Team members
must defuse any bombs that threaten the
targeted areas.

Terrorists: The Terrorist carrying the
C4 must destroy one of the given targets. 

Other Notes: There are 2 targets 
in this mission.
