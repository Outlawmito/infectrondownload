A little info on the map:

For many years 'The Vineyard' was the producer of some
of the finest wines in France. Grape vines thrived
on the mountain slopes and the grapes that grew on them
were later used in the production of wine. Business was
very successful and the owners of the property did very
well for themselves. Eventually 'The Vineyard' was
struck by a series of "natural" disasters which forced
the owners out of business and eventually away from
the large mountain villa altogether.

Some while later a terrorist force used this secluded
spot as a hideaway and location ideal to take hostage
four of the top scientists working on a high profile
military project. The terrorists have threatened to
kill the four scientists unless it is agreed by the 
French government that the project be terminated at
once.

Like most governemts, the French decided they would not
weaken to terrorist pressure and have set up an elite
force of counter terrorists to infiltrate the villa and
rescue all of the scientists. The only thing they want
terminated is the terrorist threat - the project will
continue as planned.

Terrorists: Prevent the counters from infiltrating the
hideout and rescuing the hostages. Do not kill any
hostages - they are needed as a bargaining tool and as
protection from an all out attack. Eliminate all Counter
Terrorists to make the French Government see sense.

Counter Terrorists: Rescue the hostages without putting
them at risk. The hostages must be rescued at all costs
or the project will suffer delays which cannot be
afforded. Use brutal force to gain access to the
hostages, eliminate Terrorists as neccessary.
