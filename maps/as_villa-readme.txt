* LEVEL DESCRIPTION *
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Background: After numerous assassination attempts by Spanish Nationalists have been made, the new political leader of Spain has been advised to fake his own death in order to survive.

Counter-Terrorists: Escort the President to the waterfall where he must make the leap of faith. The VIP has been given basic arms training, and is equipped with a standard combat knife and special breathing apparatus. Just make sure everything runs smoothly...

Terrorists: Our double agent has tipped us off on the President's whereabouts and his plans, this is it...
