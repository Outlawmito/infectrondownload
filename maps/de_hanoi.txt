Hanoi - Bomb/Defuse
by Tommy (http://www.youtube.com/user/T0mmyPL)
Classic Car prefab by Corvalho (modified by me :D)

Background: Fight in the infamous prison camp.
It is a medium/large sized map, with a prison
inside and a barricaded street along the outside.
Taking place at night, the map is quite dark.
The location of the map is a North Vietnamese
Army prison, based on the real-life Hanoi Hilton.

Counter-Terrorists: Prevent Terrorists
from planting a bomb.
Team members must defuse any bombs 
that threaten targeted areas.

Terrorists: The Terrorist carrying the
C4 must destroy one of the bomb
sites. 

Other Notes: There are 2 bomb
targets in the mission.
