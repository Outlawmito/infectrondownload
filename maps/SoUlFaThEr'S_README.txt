------Installation--------------------------------------

Unzip all files somewhere, then copy the folders into your Half-Life/cstrike directory

The 3 .wav files go into Halflife/cstrike/sound/ambience
The .bsp and .txt go into Halflife/cstrike/maps
The .tga files go into Halflife/cstrike/gfx/env
The .pwf and .pxp are only for PODbot users, and go into Halflife/cstrike/PODbot/WPTDefault
