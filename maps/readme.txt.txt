fy_iceicebaby by [TrK]JunkyardJack
 
This is a deathmatch style map with a small file size for a speedy download.
The map was patterned after a tic-tac-toe grid for good crossfire action.
Once you spawn you have two options:
you can either climb one of two ladders in spawn area to above for sniping fun
or exit snow base out the front for ground level combat.
You will find a buyzone on center roof above hostages if you must have AWP.
 
This map has hostages entombed in ice.
If you break ice while being careful not to shoot hostage you can rescue them at the CT base.
Good luck trying to rescue hostages, gameplay is so fast that you will probably get a metal shampoo if you try.
 
Bodybag counts will be high. Holds up to 24 players. r_speeds are good. Download it and kick the tires.
