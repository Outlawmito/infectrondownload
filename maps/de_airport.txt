Airport - Bomb/Defuse
Made by Foofinoo

Map and Custom Textures by Foofinoo
Normal Textures by VALVe



Terrorists like Airports, they can explode, kill, hijack...
But Skateboarders like Airports too. They can grind, do some tricks or create combos...
And what Terrorists hate the most are skateboarders...
Well, to piss them off, Terrorists decide to explode the Airport (I know, it's not really smart) !! 
But Counter-Terrorists are there to stop them !!



Terrorists : 
Explode the Airport !! It will piss them off !!

Counter-Terrorists :
Prevent the Terrorists bombing the Airport

NOTES : The map take place in the "Airport" map, in Tony Hawk's games (for me, THUG2)...
It's not EXACTLY the same at 100%, but the layout is the same...
